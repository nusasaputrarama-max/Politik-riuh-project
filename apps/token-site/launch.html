Launch

Not launched

There is no launch date and no countdown. The token has not been created on any network, and mainnet is not launched. This page will change only when a gate below is actually cleared — with evidence in the public repository.

Mainnet gates

Five gates, all pending

Mainnet launch requires every gate below, in order. No gate is skipped, and no earlier approval implies a later one.

  1. 01

    Concept approved by the founder

    Token name and concept decided by Rama. Candidates under discussion are recorded in the public design doc; none is final.

    Status — Pending
  2. 02

    Devnet mint + test evidence

    Token created on Solana devnet with a fresh wallet and faucet SOL, with mint address and transaction evidence recorded in the repository.

    Status — Pending
  3. 03

    Independent audit of the token plan

    ChatGPT audits the venue, supply, distribution, and anti-scam plan before anything moves to mainnet.

    Status — Pending
  4. 04

    Funding decision

    The founder decides funding for creation — about 0.02 SOL for a pump.fun creation and first buy.

    Status — Pending
  5. 05

    Founder's explicit mainnet go-ahead

    A separate, explicit go-ahead from Rama. No earlier message or approval implies this step.

    Status — Pending

Devnet plan

What happens before mainnet

On devnet: a fresh Solana wallet, free devnet SOL from the faucet, an SPL mint with metadata, and the mint address plus transaction evidence recorded in the repository. Devnet tokens have no value, are not traded, and are not distributed.

Out of scope in this phase

What will not happen

No presale, no airdrop, no liquidity provision, no token sale, no custody, and no movement of funds in this phase.
