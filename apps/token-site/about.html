About

Independent politics, read out loud

Media politik independen. Membaca isu, menguji narasi, membongkar kepentingan. Berita, analisis, opini, dan satire.

POLITIK RIUH is an independent Indonesian political ecosystem. It monitors political news and issues, verifies sources before calling anything verified, and makes room for satire and meme culture — without letting the jokes pretend to be news.

The project is not affiliated with any political party or public figure, and it does not speak for any of them. Editorial decisions are made by people, not by token holders, not by an algorithm, and not by whoever shouts loudest.

The five parts

What the project does

  • News monitoring

    Political news and issues tracked as they develop, from public sources.

  • Source verification

    Claims are checked against evidence. A claim without provenance stays unverified.

  • Satire & memes

    Political satire in its own clearly labeled space. Satire is never presented as fact.

  • One meme token

    A single Solana community token. It is subordinate to the editorial mission and never influences it.

  • Transparency

    Allocations, treasury, decisions, and corrections published where anyone can check them.

Risks and limitations

What this project cannot promise

Verification is human-led and slow; being second and right beats being first and wrong. The project can still get things wrong — when it does, corrections are published, not hidden.

Satire can be misunderstood out of context. That is why it is labeled in the image and in the caption, and kept out of the news feed.

The token is a meme artifact, not an investment. It can lose all value, it represents no equity or governance rights, and owning it grants no control over editorial decisions.

Nothing on this site is investment advice, and no regulatory compliance is claimed.

Scope

What this site is not

This is the token launch site — not the political-news portal. It does not automate any social account, and it does not post on the founder's behalf.
