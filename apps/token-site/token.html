Token

One Solana meme token

A community artifact for the POLITIK RIUH community — not an investment product, not a share in the project, and not a claim on revenue. The token is subordinate to the editorial mission: it must never influence editorial decisions.

Risk disclosure

Meme tokens are volatile and can lose all value. This token does not represent equity, governance rights, or affiliation with any party or public figure, and it carries no profit promise. There is no presale, no whitelist sale, and no guaranteed allocation. Never send funds or share a seed phrase with anyone claiming to sell this token — the official mint address, when one exists, will be published only in the public repository and on official project channels.
